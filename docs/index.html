The Snurp loop

1
Someone trades $SNURPEvery buy or sell on Pons pays a 1% pool fee (70% of it goes to the creator wallet) plus a 1% builder tax.
2
Snurp eats the feeThe creator wallet is Snurp's stomach. Every claim of the pool-fee share goes straight back into buying $SNURP.
3
Snurp burns what it boughtBought tokens are sent to the dead address. Gone forever. Every burn is public on Blockscout.
4
Repeat, foreverLiquidity is locked at graduation and can never be withdrawn. Volume feeds Snurp; Snurp shrinks supply.
Rule of Snurp: 100% of the pool-fee share → buy $SNURP → burn. The 1% builder tax is the only thing the team keeps, and it is written into the launch on chain. Dev never sells. Dev tokens only ever move to the burn address. The bot that does it is open source; every burn is a public transaction.

In OKX Wallet, MetaMask or Rabby, add the network below. It's an Arbitrum-stack L2, gas is paid in ETH and costs almost nothing.

Open the $SNURP page on pons.family, pick an amount, approve in your wallet. Liquidity is locked forever once the curve graduates.
